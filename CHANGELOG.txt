MINI&ME FOOTBALL CLUB CHANGELOG

Current Stable Baseline: v2.12.0 Last Updated: 17 August 2026

v2.12.0 — STABLE BASELINE 17 August 2026

• Declared the current game the formal stable development baseline. •
Removed and disabled the temporary developer Quick Sim feature. •
Changed managed national teams to use an AI-selected default 23-player
squad. • Added AI best-fit starting XI behaviour for managed nations. •
GM national-team involvement is now optional rather than mandatory. •
Manual call-ups and squad changes are retained as GM overrides. •
National squad alerts are advisory rather than a mandatory simulation
barrier. • Retained hidden nation-strength tiers for international
simulation. • Retained World Cup and qualification systems developed
through the v2.11 series. • Future development emphasis changed to bug
fixing, AI, gameplay logic, UX, UI, GUI, performance and displayed-data
improvements.

RECENT v2.11 DEVELOPMENT THEMES

International Football • Expanded World Cup qualification across the six
federations. • Added qualification tables, federation views and
qualification statuses. • Improved qualification-cycle state handling
and legacy-save safeguards. • Added wider Global Qualification Race
visibility. • Improved international result recency and presentation. •
Added national-team management and squad confirmation workflows. • Added
World Cup group-stage and knockout-round progression. • Added World Cup
snapshots to Season Review. • Paused domestic league progression during
active World Cup tournament periods.

Simulation Realism • Introduced an internal national-team power model. •
Added nation tiers to support more credible international results. •
Removed nation-tier labels from the player-facing interface. • Tuned
major football nations to remain appropriately competitive. • Reduced
implausible high-margin upsets involving established powers. •
Maintained the possibility of credible upsets and emerging nations.

UX and Presentation • Improved International section navigation. •
Separated club international and national international information. •
Improved World Cup qualification visibility. • Added managed-nation
highlighting. • Improved national-team action routing. • Improved Season
Review international reporting. • Removed stale or duplicated World Cup
information where identified. • Refined Home-screen international action
placement.

NOTE

The v2.11 series contained many iterative test builds. This changelog
records the principal systems that now form the v2.12.0 stable baseline
rather than attempting to reproduce every experimental patch number.

From v2.12.0 onward, each release should be recorded individually.
