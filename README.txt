MINI&ME FOOTBALL CLUB v2.1 ALPHA MOBILE
Club Development Update

New:
- Four Level 1–5 facilities: Training Centre, Youth Academy, Scouting Department and Medical Centre.
- Facility upgrades require club cash and increase annual operating costs.
- Training Centre now influences offseason development and age-related decline.
- Youth Academy produces 1–3 club prospects each offseason depending on level.
- Scouting Department controls the uncertainty shown around player potential.
- Medical Centre establishes the infrastructure for the upcoming injury/availability system.
- Offseason player development now varies by age, potential, coach quality and Training Centre level.
- Last offseason development is shown on the Club screen.
- Existing careers automatically migrate their old Training Centre into the new facility model.
- v2.0.4 top navigation design retained.
- Existing saves remain compatible.
